void Fish::grow ( double  tsb,
double  temp 
)

Implement growth, i.e., set new length and effective GSI after 1 year of growth.

Parameters
tsbTotal stock biomass during the growing season [kT i.e. 10^6 kg]
tempTemperature during the growing season (deg C)

In Dankel et al model, this function does nothing. New length is set by set_age().

In Joshi et al, we first calculate \(\Delta l_p\), and then increment length based on whether the individual is a juvenile or adult. We also calculate the effective GSI and set the new length.

Here, two length increments are calculated based on tsb: potential increment is when tsb = 0, i.e., there is not density constraint on growth. real increment is calculated using the actual tsb. Actual length increment is based on the real increment. Potential increment is for analysis purposes.

This function does not increment age as growth can happen during the beginning of the year. Age is incremented separately in the population update at the end of the year.

◆ produceRecruits()

double Fish::produceRecruits ( double  ssb,
double  temp 
)

Calculate the number of surviving recruits produced based on egg production and offspring survival until recruitment.

Parameters
ssbTotal spawning stock biomass \(S\) of the population (kg)
tempTemperature (deg C)

In Dankel et al model, the number of recruits is calculated directly as \(r_0 w\).

In Joshi et al, the number of eggs produced is first calculated based on the effective GSI. The number of surviving eggs is then calculated by applying two survival probabilities:

  • \(s_0\) is the survival probability of offspring until recruitment
  • \(1/(1+S/B_{1/2})\) is the probability of survival during recruitment. This is modelled as a Beverton-Holt function.

Formulas:

For the BevertonHoltDirect model:

\[ \text{recruits} = r_0 \cdot w \cdot \frac{1}{1 + \frac{\text{ssb}}{B_{\text{half}}}} \]

For the RickerDirect model:

\[ \text{recruits} = r_0 \cdot w \cdot \exp(\beta_4 \cdot (T - T_{\text{ref}})) \cdot 2^{-\frac{\text{ssb}}{B_{\text{half}}}} \]

For the BevertonHoltBioenergetic model:

\[ \text{eggs} = \text{fecundity}(w, \delta, \text{gsi}_{\text{effective}}) \]

\[ \text{recruits} = \text{eggs} \cdot s_0 \cdot \frac{1}{1 + \frac{\text{ssb}}{B_{\text{half}}}} \]

For the RickerBioenergetic model:

\[ \text{eggs} = \text{fecundity}(w, \delta, \text{gsi}_{\text{effective}}) \]

\[ \text{recruits} = \text{eggs} \cdot s_0 \cdot \exp(\beta_4 \cdot (T - T_{\text{ref}})) \cdot 2^{-\frac{\text{ssb}}{B_{\text{half}}}} \]

Exceptions
std::runtime_errorIf an invalid recruitment model is specified.
